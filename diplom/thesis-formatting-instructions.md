# Agent Instructions: Thesis Formatting Requirements (DUIKT)

Instruction for an AI agent that writes qualification (diploma) works according to the regulations of the State University of Information and Communication Technologies (DUIKT), Kyiv, 2023.

**Source:** Положення про кваліфікаційні роботи здобувачів вищої освіти у ДУІКТ

---

## 1. General

| Parameter | Bachelor | Master |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Scope (pages)** | 50–70 | 70–90 |
| **References** | ≥ 20 sources | ≥ 30 sources |
| **Language** | Ukrainian (default) | Ukrainian (default) |
| **Standard** | ДСТУ 3008:2015 | ДСТУ 3008:2015 |

**Important:**
- List of references and appendices do NOT count toward page limits.
- Work must be in Ukrainian unless a foreign language is expressly approved.
- Plagiarism is prohibited; all borrowed material must be cited.
- Work must pass plagiarism check and be placed in the university repository.

---

## 2. Typography and Layout

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Font** | Times New Roman, 14 pt |
| **Line spacing** | 1.5 |
| **Paper** | A4 (210 × 297 mm) |
| **Text color** | Black, medium weight |

### Margins (mm)
- Left: 25
- Right: 10
- Top: 20
- Bottom: 20

### Paragraph
- Indent: 5 characters (constant throughout the text)

---

## 3. Document Structure (Order)

1. Титульний аркуш (Title page)
2. Завдання на кваліфікаційну роботу (Assignment)
3. Реферат (Abstract)
4. Реферат англійською (Abstract in English) — for Master only
5. Відгук наукового керівника (Supervisor’s review)
6. Рецензія (External review)
7. ЗМІСТ (Table of contents)
8. ПЕРЕЛІК УМОВНИХ ПОЗНАЧЕНЬ (List of symbols) — if needed
9. ВСТУП (Introduction)
10. РОЗДІЛ 1 (Chapter 1)
11. РОЗДІЛ 2 (Chapter 2)
12. РОЗДІЛ 3 (Chapter 3)
13. ВИСНОВКИ (Conclusions)
14. ПЕРЕЛІК ПОСИЛАНЬ (References)
15. ДОДАТКИ (Appendices) — if needed
16. ПРЕЗЕНТАЦІЯ (Presentation slides)
17. Короткий звіт подібності (Plagiarism report)

---

## 4. Headings

### Chapters (e.g. РОЗДІЛ 1, РОЗДІЛ 2)
- Centered
- UPPERCASE
- Each chapter starts on a **new page**
- No period at the end of heading
- No hyphenation in headings
- Spacing: 2 line spaces before and after
- Do not underline headings

### Subchapters (e.g. 1.1, 1.2)
- Centered
- Sentence case (only first letter capitalized)
- Start with 1 line space after the previous text (same page)
- Numbering: chapter.subsection (e.g. 2.1, 2.2)

### General
- Do not place a heading at the bottom of a page if only one line of text follows.
- Minimum 2 line spaces between heading and adjacent text.

---

## 5. Page Numbering

- Continuous Arabic numerals throughout the document
- Position: **top right**
- Do **not** number:
  - Титульний аркуш
  - ЗАВДАННЯ
  - РЕФЕРАТ
  - ЗМІСТ
  - ВСТУП
  - ВИСНОВКИ
  - ПЕРЕЛІК ПОСИЛАНЬ

---

## 6. Abstract (Реферат)

Must include:
1. **Scope:** total pages, number of figures, tables, references (including appendices).
2. **Object of study** (об’єкт дослідження)
3. **Subject of study** (предмет дослідження)
4. **Goal and tasks**
5. **Research methods** and their results
6. **Short summary of work** — tasks solved (e.g. "досліджено", "показано", "простежено", "виявлено", "окреслено", "визначено", "обґрунтовано", "встановлено")
7. **Keywords:** 8–10 words, nominative case, UPPERCASE, at the end

Style: concise, scientific, avoid complex constructions.

---

## 7. Introduction (ВСТУП)

Volume: **3–5 pages**

Required sections in order:
1. **Актуальність теми** — relevance for science and practice
2. **Аналіз останніх досліджень** — review of prior work, authors, unsolved parts
3. **Мета і завдання** — goal and tasks (goal ≠ "Дослідження..." or "Вивчення...")
4. **Об’єкт дослідження** — start with: «Об'єктом дослідження є ...»
5. **Предмет дослідження** — narrower scope within the object
6. **Методи дослідження** — methods used and what they were used for
7. **Наукова новизна** — novelty (object, subject, method, system)
8. **Практична значущість** — practical applicability
9. **Апробація** — conferences, publications (if any)
10. **Теоретична/методична значущість** — broader significance
11. **Структура роботи** — brief overview of chapters

---

## 8. Main Chapters (Content Requirements)

### РОЗДІЛ 1 (Theoretical-methodological)
- Review of literature and theory
- Critical analysis of sources
- Methodologies that will be used later

### РОЗДІЛ 2 (Analytical-research)
- Empirical data
- Analysis of real-world material
- Period: at least **2 years** of data
- Use of methods from Chapter 1

### РОЗДІЛ 3 (Constructive/Proposals)
- Concrete proposals/recommendations
- Based on results of Chapters 1 and 2
- May use forecasting methods (qualitative/quantitative)

**Note:** Each chapter must have at least 2 subsections.

---

## 9. Conclusions (ВИСНОВКИ)

- Point-by-point summary of results
- Practical recommendations
- Feasibility and future use of results

---

## 10. References (ПЕРЕЛІК ПОСИЛАНЬ)

Two allowed variants:
1. **Order of appearance** in the text (recommended)
2. **Grouped:** legislation first → Ukrainian sources (alphabetically) → foreign sources (alphabetically)

---

## 11. Tables

- Place after first mention in the text (or on next page)
- Numbering: `Chapter.Number` (e.g. Table 2.1, Table 3.2)
- Caption: above table, sentence case
- All tables must be cited in the text
- For continuation: «Продовження таблиці X.X»
- Column headers: singular
- No period at the end of headers
- If term appears < 3 times, explain in text only, do not add to list of symbols

---

## 12. Figures (Рисунки)

- Place after first mention in the text (or on next page)
- Numbering: `Fig. Chapter.Number` (e.g. Рис. 1.1, Рис. 2.3)
- Caption: below figure, centered
- Caption must be on the same page as the figure
- No period at the end of caption
- All figures must be cited in the text (e.g. рис. 1.1, табл. 4.2)
- If figure is from another author, cite the source
- Do not use 2D/3D charts if extra dimensions add no information

---

## 13. Formulas and Equations

- Use MS Equation (or equivalent)
- Placed after first mention in text
- Centered, 1 line space above and below
- Numbering: (Chapter.Number), e.g. (3.2), right-aligned
- Latin letters in formulas: italic (except functions: sin, cos, lg, exp, tg, min)
- Symbol explanations: below formula, indented, in order of appearance
- Provide source references for formulas
- Units: use SI (V, A, Ω, F, H, m, s, etc.)
- Calculation order: formula → substituted values → result with units

---

## 14. Lists

- Introduce with colon
- Level 1: lowercase letters with parenthesis (a), б), в)) or dash (—)
- Level 2: Arabic numerals with parenthesis (1), 2), 3))
- Level 1: paragraph indent
- Level 2: additional indent relative to level 1

---

## 15. Notes (Примітки)

- Single note: «Примітка.» + text
- Multiple: «Примітки:» then numbered list

---

## 16. List of Symbols (ПЕРЕЛІК УМОВНИХ ПОЗНАЧЕНЬ)

- Optional
- Used for abbreviations, symbols, specific terms
- Two columns: term | full form
- Alphabetical order
- Do not include terms used less than 3 times (explain in text instead)

---

## 17. Appendices (ДОДАТКИ)

- Intermediate calculations
- Raw data
- Large tables
- Auxiliary material

---

## 18. Computer Programs

If software was developed:
- Algorithm flowchart
- Program listing
- Test run
- Programming language
- User manual

---

## 19. Academic Integrity

- No plagiarism
- Every quote and borrowed idea must have a source
- Work must pass university plagiarism check
- Plagiarism report is last page (not numbered, not in page count)

---

## 20. Presentation for Defense

- 12–15 slides
- Complement the oral presentation
- Duration of defense: max 30 minutes, presentation 7–10 minutes
- Include: relevance, object, subject, goal, tasks, main results, conclusions, proposals
